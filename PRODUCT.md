# teli.ca: product context

Read this before building or changing any page. The visual system lives in DESIGN.md.

## Brand

- Name: teli.ca. Wordmark is "teli" in ink with ".ca" in the green accent.
- Use teli.ca everywhere: page titles, footer, copyright line, Open Graph site name
- Hero, two lines: "Tech, sorted." large in black, then "The IT person you don't have." smaller in gray
- The owner's name and photo do not appear anywhere on the site. The About page describes the day job without naming the employer.
- Legal or registered business name: not decided yet

## What it is

An independent, one-person IT consulting and hands-on technical services practice based in Toronto, with over 25 years of experience. Senior, practical help without hiring an IT department.

- Work is mostly one-off: a project, a fix or a setup. Ongoing help is available on request, but there is no published ongoing plan.
- Remote, or on-site in Toronto when it's better in person.
- Scheduled around a full-time role, often evenings and weekends. The site says so plainly.

## Who it's for

- Small businesses, roughly 5 to 50 staff
- Nonprofits and associations
- Solo professionals and home offices
- Individuals and households

Copy speaks to "you" and never assumes the reader has an office.

## The one goal

Every page leads to the single contact form at /contact. No phone number, no street address and no email address on the site.

## Voice and copy rules

- Plain, direct and warm. Say what the reader can do and what improves for them.
- Canadian spelling.
- No em dashes.
- No hype words: supercharge, world-class, enterprise-grade, seamless, cutting-edge.
- No "Not X. Y." slogans, no calling things "theater".
- No scale signals that don't fit a one-person practice: logo walls, "trusted by 1000+", rankings.
- Sentence case. One label per block, never repeated.
- No commitments on pricing, response times or availability.

## SEO requirements

- Unique title and meta description on every page, titles end with "| teli.ca"
- One primary search target per service page, as listed above, Toronto-qualified where it fits
- One h1 per page, no skipped heading levels
- sitemap.xml (generated by @astrojs/sitemap), robots.txt, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags and a sharing image
- Structured data built in src/data/schema.ts: the business on the home page; service, FAQ and breadcrumbs on service pages
- No city-by-city landing pages unless each has genuinely different content
- Google Business Profile and Bing Places as a service-area business, address hidden
- Measurement: Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Cloudflare Web Analytics (script-based, works on any host)
- After launch: consider a small Guides section answering common questions

## AI assistants

- Decision: all crawlers are allowed, including AI training crawlers. This is a sales site; being known to AI assistants is the goal. Meta bundles training and indexing in one crawler, and blocking Google-Extended limits Gemini, so blocking training would cost visibility.
- Pages are plain static HTML, readable without JavaScript.
- /llms.txt is generated from src/data/services.ts by src/pages/llms.txt.ts.
- Bing Webmaster Tools matters: Copilot uses Bing's index, and other assistants draw on Bing and Google results.

## Contact form

- One form for everyone: name, email, message. Nothing else.
- Spam protection: a hidden honeypot field, plus Cloudflare Turnstile
- The form posts to a small Cloudflare Worker on a workers.dev address (worker/ folder). The Worker checks Turnstile and the honeypot, then sends the message through Resend.
- Resend sends from notify.teli.ca, with the visitor's address as Reply-To.
- The destination mailbox and the Resend API key are Worker secrets. Never commit them.
- The sending address is on the destination mailbox's Safe senders list so submissions never land in Junk.

## DNS and email rules

- DNS for teli.ca must stay at GoDaddy. The domain's email depends on it, and moving nameservers would break it.
- Never change or remove these records: MX, the SPF TXT record on the root, the _outlook TXT record, the autodiscover CNAME, the _domainconnect CNAME.
- Resend's records go on the notify subdomain only. Do not edit the root SPF record for Resend.
- DMARC starts at p=none (monitor only). Tighten only after reports confirm all legitimate mail aligns.
- The four A records on the root point to GitHub Pages. www should be a CNAME to misoverstood.github.io.

## Privacy and security

- The form never asks for passwords, account details or ID, and says so.
- No third-party trackers, no cookies, no consent banner needed
- The privacy policy at /privacy names Cloudflare, Resend, Microsoft and GitHub. Update it whenever a processor or analytics changes.
- This repo is public: never commit secrets, private email addresses or client information
